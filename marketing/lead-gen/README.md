# Stehlen Auto — Net-New Lead Generation Plan
## Acquisition Only | Beyond the Existing Asset Base

**Author:** Marcus Steel, Marketing Director
**Date:** June 2026
**Scope:** Net-new customer acquisition — channels and tactics NOT currently
active. This document does NOT cover reactivation of the 36,738-contact Champions
list (that is a retention/reactivation play, already documented in
`marketing/plans/`). Everything here is ACQUISITION: people who have never
bought from Stehlen and have no existing relationship with the brand.

**Strategic context:** GA4 shows ChatGPT is already a top-3 traffic source,
meaning GEO is working for branded queries. The gap is unbranded intent — "where
can I buy a good no-drill tonneau cover for a 2021 F-150" returns RealTruck,
Amazon, AutoZone, Walmart, BAK, Rough Country. Not Stehlen. Closing that gap is
the underlying mission of every channel below.

## The First $5,000

If I had $5,000 and needed to spend it Monday morning to generate net-new
customers, here is the exact allocation:

1. **$2,250 — Google Shopping** (30 days at $75/day)
   Tonneau covers + grilles as hero SKUs. Feed must have YMM-formatted titles
   before launch — this is a 4-hour task, do it first. ROAS floor: 2.86x.
   Expected: 30-60 net-new customers at $37-$75 CAC.

2. **$750 — Google Search** (brand defense + part number campaigns)
   "$10/day brand defense is non-negotiable. Competitors are bidding on
   'stehlen tonneau cover' right now. $15/day part number exact match."
   Expected: 15-30 net-new customers at $25-$50 CAC.

3. **$1,000 — Content production** (2 buyer-guide posts + 1 install video)
   "2021-2023 Ford F-150 Soft Tri-Fold Tonneau Cover: What Fits, What Doesn't,
   What We Recommend" = one buying-guide post. Film the install video the same
   day. Both seed Google index AND ChatGPT/Perplexity citation pool.

4. **$800 — Insert cards**
   Ship with every eBay/Amazon fulfillment. 10,000 cards/mo, 2-4% conversion
   rate = 200-400 inbound DTC-attributed sessions at $0.08 CAC for the click.

5. **$200 — Zero-cost authority amplification**
   Post install-guide excerpts in FordF150Forum, TacomaWorld, RamForum.
   Use the forum content to seed backlinks. No budget needed, but reserve
   $200 for expedited domain-authority tools (Ahrefs/Semrush trial) to
   identify the three highest-value backlink targets in month 1.

**Total: $5,000. Expected output: 60-120 net-new customers in 30 days.**
That is a blended CAC of $42-$83 — within the $55-$120 paid-blend benchmark
and meaningfully below the $180 LTV floor for even a one-time buyer.
