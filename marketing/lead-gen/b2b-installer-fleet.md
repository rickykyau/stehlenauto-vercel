## The Play

The CB data shows $516K in existing wholesale orders and 139 accounts with
10+ lifetime orders averaging $134,490 LTV each. These are the most valuable
customers Stehlen has ever had, and they have never been actively marketed to
as a B2B segment.

Converting even 10% of those 139 accounts to a direct wholesale relationship
on stehlenauto.com = 14 accounts at $134K average LTV = $1.87M in estimated
future revenue with near-zero acquisition cost.

The B2B channel is anti-seasonal: installer shops buy throughout the year,
fleet accounts accelerate in Q4 (year-end fleet purchasing cycle), and the
predictable recurring revenue smooths the DTC seasonal curve. When consumer
demand dips in September-February, B2B holds the revenue floor.

The go-to-market splits into two distinct sub-channels with different timelines,
different buyers, and different acquisition tactics.

## Sub-Channel 1: Independent Installer Shops

### The Buyer Profile

Owner-operated truck accessory shops and auto customization shops. They install
tonneau covers, grilles, bull bars, running boards, and hitches for their
customers. They buy at wholesale and sell at retail — the markup is their margin.
They evaluate new suppliers on: (1) product availability and lead time,
(2) fitment accuracy, (3) wholesale pricing terms, (4) return policy for
damaged/wrong-fit items.

They are NOT interested in: brand story, paid social, or email sequences.
They are interested in a simple dealer price sheet, phone access to someone
who can answer fitment questions, and net-30 terms after an initial order.

### Identifying the Right Shops

**From CB data (first priority — zero outreach cost, highest conversion rate):**
- Pull all CB accounts with 10+ lifetime orders, primarily fulfilled via
  eBay or Amazon FBA, with multiple different vehicle makes in their order
  history (not a single consumer with multiple vehicles — a shop buys across
  makes)
- These are buyers who have already purchased Stehlen products. They know
  the product. The pitch is: "Buy direct and save 15-30% vs. your current
  marketplace price."
- Extract contact information if available (business name, phone, address from
  order records)

**From Google Maps (for geographic expansion):**
- Search: "truck accessories installer [city]", "truck customization shop [city]",
  "auto accessories installation [city]"
- Top priority metros (by Stehlen CB revenue geography): Los Angeles/IE,
  Houston, Dallas/Fort Worth, Phoenix, Atlanta, Denver, Tampa, San Diego
- Map density: target shops within 50 miles of the Walnut CA warehouse first
  (same-day delivery is a competitive advantage for nearby accounts)

**From industry directories:**
- SEMA member directory (filterable by "truck accessories" and "installation")
- Total Truck Centers dealer network (national installer network)
- Keystone Automotive installer database (if accessible)
- 4WheelParts/Discount Tire commercial partner lists

### Outreach Sequence

**Step 1: Phone call (highest response rate for shop owners)**
Script: "Hi, this is [name] from Stehlen Auto. We're the manufacturer
behind some of the grilles, tonneau covers, and bull bars you may have
seen on Amazon or eBay — we're based in Walnut, CA. We're building out our
dealer program and wanted to see if you'd be open to a quick conversation
about wholesale pricing on the accessories you're already installing."

Do NOT send a cold email first. Shop owners are volume-email-averse.
Phone call first, email follow-up second.

**Step 2: Email with dealer price sheet**
Send within 24 hours of a positive phone conversation.
Subject: "Stehlen Auto Dealer Pricing — [Make] accessories wholesale"
Include: 1-page dealer pricing overview (PDF), top 20 SKUs for their
primary makes, net-30 terms overview, returns policy for dealer accounts.

**Step 3: Sample shipment**
For interested shops: ship 1-2 samples at no cost. A shop owner who installs
a Stehlen tonneau cover themselves is a sold dealer. This is the fastest
path to a purchase order.

**Step 4: Onboarding**
First 3 orders: prepayment (net-0 until trust is established)
Orders 4+: Net-30 terms
Volume tiers:
- Bronze: $0-$1,000/mo — standard wholesale pricing (30% off MSRP)
- Silver: $1,000-$3,000/mo — 35% off MSRP
- Gold: $3,000+/mo — 40% off MSRP + dedicated account manager access
- Platinum: $5,000+/mo — 45% off MSRP + early access to new products

### B2B Infrastructure on Stehlenauto.com

The `/pro` or `/wholesale` page needs to exist before outreach begins.
Dealer prospects will Google "stehlen auto dealer" after a phone call —
a landing page legitimizes the program.

**Page content:**
- Simple dealer application form (name, business name, phone, primary
  vehicle makes installed, monthly volume estimate)
- 3-tier pricing grid (without exposing exact prices — "Request dealer
  pricing" CTA)
- "Why Stehlen" for dealers: fitment guarantee, direct manufacturer, 1-3 day
  shipping from Walnut CA, returns accepted for fitment errors
- Phone number and email for dealer inquiries (a real person, not a chatbot)

**Shopify B2B pricing:** Shopify's native B2B feature (available on Shopify
Plus or via wholesale apps like SparkLayer/Wholesale Club on standard plans)
creates a separate login-gated pricing view for dealer accounts. Each dealer
account sees their tier's wholesale pricing at checkout. This is cleaner than
managing discount codes per account.

## Sub-Channel 2: Fleet Accounts

### The Buyer Profile

Procurement managers at companies with vehicle fleets that need to be outfitted:
- Construction companies (hitches, grille guards, light bars, bed mats for
  F-250/350 and Ram 2500/3500 work trucks)
- Landscaping companies (bed accessories, hitches, grille guards)
- Utility companies (running boards, grille guards, light packages for SUVs
  and trucks)
- Government/municipal (police departments, fire/rescue, public works — all
  have significant aftermarket accessory budgets)
- Delivery and logistics fleets (hitches, running boards, step bars)

Fleet buyers are different from installer shops in two ways:
- Much longer sales cycle (3-6 months, requires procurement approval)
- Much higher AOV per order ($1,500-$10,000+ per order for multi-vehicle outfitting)

### Fleet Targeting

**Priority 1 — Construction and trade companies in TX + CA:**
Both states have high construction activity, high F-250/350 and Ram 2500/3500
penetration, and large regional companies that outfit 10-50 trucks at a time.

LinkedIn Sales Navigator ($80/mo) filters by:
- Company size: 50-500 employees
- Industry: Construction, Landscaping, Utilities, Transportation
- Geography: TX (Houston, Dallas, San Antonio), CA (LA Basin, San Diego, Bay Area)
- Title: Fleet Manager, Procurement Manager, Operations Manager, Owner

**Priority 2 — Government/municipal (longer cycle, higher volume):**
Cities, counties, and state agencies buy through procurement processes that
take 3-6 months but result in orders of 20-100 units. Texas and California
both have active truck accessory budgets for public works fleets.

Note: Municipal procurement typically requires being listed on a procurement
platform (e.g., BidNet, Periscope) or qualifying as an approved vendor.
This is a month 4-6 play, not month 1.

### Fleet Outreach Approach

**LinkedIn outreach (primary for fleet buyers — they are on LinkedIn):**
Message template:
"Hi [Name],

I manage the dealer program at Stehlen Auto — we manufacture aftermarket
accessories for Ford F-series, Ram, and Chevy Silverado work trucks (hitches,
grille guards, running boards, light bars).

I noticed [Company] operates a fleet in [Location]. If you're outfitting
trucks and want to cut your per-unit cost by 30-40% vs. going through a
dealer, I'd like to show you our fleet pricing.

Are you the right person to talk to, or should I reach out to someone else
on the fleet side?

[Name], Stehlen Auto"

Key principle: fleet buyers are not excited by product features. They are
excited by price per unit, availability (lead time), and ease of ordering
(one PO for 20 units, not 20 separate orders).

**Follow-up:** If no LinkedIn response in 5 days, find direct email via
Hunter.io or Apollo.io and send a one-page fleet pricing sheet PDF.

### Fleet Pricing Structure

Different from installer pricing — fleet buyers negotiate on per-unit cost
for a committed volume.

| Fleet order size | Discount |
|-----------------|---------|
| 5-9 units | 30% off MSRP |
| 10-24 units | 35% off MSRP |
| 25-49 units | 40% off MSRP |
| 50+ units | 45% off MSRP + free freight |

Payment terms for fleet: Net-30 after credit application for first order
exceeding $1,000. Larger orders ($5,000+) may require 50% deposit.

## B2B Klaviyo Flows

Create a separate B2B segment in Klaviyo (do NOT mix with DTC consumer contacts).
Tag all B2B profiles with: Business_Type, Volume_Tier, Region, Primary_Makes.

**Dealer Welcome Series (5 emails over 14 days):**
1. Immediate: "Your dealer account is active — here's how to order"
   (account login, portal walkthrough, price sheet attached)
2. Day 3: "Your top-selling categories for [region] market"
   (regional bestsellers, what other dealers in CA/TX order most)
3. Day 7: "New product alert — first look before we announce to consumers"
   (dealers get early access 2 weeks before consumer launch)
4. Day 10: "Reorder reminder" (if no order in first 10 days)
5. Day 14: "Need help with fitment questions? Call this number directly"
   (direct manufacturer access is the key differentiator vs. distribution channel)

**Quarterly seasonal emails:**
- Pre-spring (February): "Stock up before the truck season rush — here's what
  will sell in March-May"
- Pre-fall (September): "Tonneau cover season — order by [date] for October
  readiness"
- Year-end fleet (November): "Fleet purchasing calendar — submit POs by Dec 15
  for Q4 delivery guarantee"

## KPIs

- Primary: Active dealer accounts (target 5 by month 2, 15 by month 4, 25 by month 8)
- Primary: B2B revenue as % of total DTC (target 15% by month 6, 20% by month 9)
- Primary: B2B monthly order value per active account (target $800-$1,500/mo average)
- Primary: Fleet accounts in pipeline (target 3-5 by month 4; first close by month 6-8)
- Secondary: Dealer account retention rate (target >85% — once an installer
  is buying from you, churn should be minimal if product and service hold up)
- Guardrail: Net-30 accounts receivable — monitor outstanding balances monthly.
  Cap any single account at 60 days outstanding. Total AR should not exceed 15%
  of monthly B2B revenue.
- Guardrail: Dealer pricing confidentiality — wholesale prices must not be
  accessible to non-authenticated users on stehlenauto.com (Shopify B2B
  login gating is the mechanism)
