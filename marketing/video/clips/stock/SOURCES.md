## Caveats (read before assembling)
- `01-side-drive` and `07-drift-hero` were shot on a closed test course (tire barriers,
  cones, buildings, and a "Toyota Gazoo Racing" banner appear in the WIDER parts of those
  clips). The mid-frames used here are clean, but **trim to the tight tracking moments** to
  avoid the branded banner / track furniture.
- `04-arrival` bed has a hard canopy/camper topper (not a flat tonneau). Reads "adventure
  rig" but is not the flat hard tonneau jackpot.
- No true ground-level rear-chase existed in free-commercial sources; `02-rear-chase` is an
  aerial of the truck pulling away (closest available). See report note.
