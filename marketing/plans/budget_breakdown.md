## Cash Flow Warning: Month 3-4 Gap

The ramp from $0 to $23K/month in marketing spend happens faster than revenue accumulates in the bank. Shopify payouts are 2-3 business days but B2B invoices run net-30. The worst-case cash gap is in Month 4:

- Month 4 marketing spend required: $23,139
- Month 3 cumulative revenue: $190,000
- Month 3 gross profit retained (after COGS): ~$66,800
- Month 3 marketing already spent: $29,867
- **Available cash entering Month 4:** ~$36,900
- **Month 4 spend required:** $23,139
- **Buffer:** $13,761 — tight but workable if no inventory spikes

Recommendation: Maintain a $75,000 operating reserve before scaling paid spend above $15K/month. If that reserve does not exist, secure a $50,000 line of credit before Month 2 launch.
